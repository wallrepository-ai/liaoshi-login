Connecting from Claude or another AI client: sign in once, approve the tools once.

1 · Add the connector

Add sources-liaoshi as a custom connector

In Claude open Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector, give it a name (for example sources-liaoshi) and paste this URL:

https://mzjfnyjparfdeiohlxoa.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp-server

Other MCP-capable clients (Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, and others) take the same URL. A free Claude account can hold one custom connector.

2 · Sign in

Sign in when Claude says the connector "needs setup"

Click Connect on the connector card, sign in with your email and password, and you are sent back to the chat. No account yet? Create one, click the verification link in your inbox, then connect again.

Your sign-in renews itself for 30 days. After that Claude asks you to sign in once more.

3 · The permission cards

"Claude wants to use Query from sources-liaoshi"

Claude asks before it uses any tool of a custom connector for the first time. This is Claude's own safety step, not a sign-in problem, and the database cannot switch it off. You will see one card per tool:

ToolWhat it doesChanges anything?
List tableslists the tables of the databaseno, read-only
Describe tableexplains the columns of one tableno, read-only
Queryruns one SELECT, at most 200 rowsno, read-only

Submit commentrecords a comment or correction under your nameyes, writes

Choose Always allow on the read-only tools and that card does not come back. Allow once means you are asked again next time. For Submit comment you may prefer to approve each time, so nothing is recorded in your name without you seeing it.

Set all tools at once

Open Customize → Connectors, choose the connector and look for Tools or Tool permissions. There each tool, or the read-only group, can be set to Always allow; some plans also offer Skip approvals for the whole connector. Only do this for connectors you trust, which for a read-only research database is a reasonable choice.

Asked again after a while?

When the database server is updated, Claude forgets earlier approvals and asks once more. Choose Always allow again; nothing else is needed.

Trouble?

Common messages

"Its setup isn't finished": sign in through the connector card, then repeat your question.

"Your login session expired": close the sign-in window and reconnect from the chat client; the sign-in page is valid for 10 minutes.

"Please verify your email first": click the link in the verification email, then sign in again.

"Rate limit reached": 60 calls per hour per account; wait a little and continue.

Inside a chat, type menu for the info menu, or read it on the menu page. Back to sign-in.
